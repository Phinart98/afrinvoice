# Invoice Passport: weekly numbers

The target, set 2026-10-20: by **2026-12-15**, reach either of these:
- **≥5 paid Pro unlocks**;
- **≥300 `generate_invoice` events with ≥30 `pro_checkout_click`**.

What each result means:
- **≥300 downloads but almost no Pro interest:** change the price, the offer or the channel.
- **Fewer than 100 downloads:** a discovery problem. Fix distribution, not the product.
